Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All data you enter in KoreGrid is stored locally on your iOS device, encrypted at rest by iOS. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. KoreGrid requires no account, no signup, and no internet connection to use. Everything runs on-device.

Does KoreGrid connect to my brokerage?

No. KoreGrid is intentionally not a trading platform. There is no execution, no custody, and no live market data. You enter your data yourself.

How do I back up my data?

Use the in-app JSON export feature to save a full backup of your journal. You can store this file wherever you like — iCloud Drive, Files, your computer, etc.

Can I restore data on a new device?

Yes. Use the in-app import feature and select a previously saved JSON export to restore your journal on another device.

Why does the App request Camera / Photo Library access?

Only when you choose to attach a receipt photo to a transaction. The permission is requested at the moment of use, and photos are saved with your transaction record on-device. Nothing is uploaded.

Will my data be lost if I delete the App?

Yes, deleting the App removes its local storage. We recommend exporting your journal regularly as your own backup.

Is KoreGrid investment advice?

No. KoreGrid is a journal and calculator. It does not provide investment advice, recommendations, or research. See our in-app disclaimer and Terms of Use.
