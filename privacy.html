Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how KoreGrid ("we," "our," or "us") handles your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Summary

KoreGrid does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. All journal entries, transactions, holdings, and receipt photos you create in the App are kept locally on your device and are never sent to us or any third party.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No personal identifying information (name, email, phone, address).
  • No account, login, or signup data.
  • No tracking identifiers, advertising IDs, or device fingerprinting.
  • No analytics about how you use the App are sent off-device.
  • No location, contacts, calendar, or other device-permission data beyond what is required for features you explicitly use (e.g., camera and photo library access when attaching a receipt).

Data Stored on Your Device

KoreGrid stores all user-created content locally on your iOS device, protected by iOS encryption at rest. This includes:

  • Holdings, transactions, and notes you enter.
  • Receipt photos you attach from the camera or photo library.
  • App preferences and settings.

Because this data resides solely on your device, it is your responsibility to safeguard it. We encourage regular exports of the data to keep your own backup.

Permissions

  • Camera & Photo Library: Used only when you choose to attach a receipt photo to a transaction. Photos you attach are saved with your transaction record on-device. We do not upload, scan, or analyze their contents.
  • No other permissions: KoreGrid does not request location, microphone, contacts, or tracking permissions.

Data Export

You may export all of your journal data at any time using the in-app export feature. The export is a JSON file produced on-device. Where you store or transfer that file is your choice.

Children's Privacy

KoreGrid is not directed to children under 14. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we collect no information at all, this applies automatically.

Third-Party Services

KoreGrid does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising networks, social plugins, or SDKs that collect user data. The App does not connect to brokerages, exchanges, or live market data feeds.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Because we collect no data, no notification mechanism is needed.
