Built for serious investors

Everything you need to understand your portfolio — and nothing you don't.

📊

Dashboard

Net worth, unrealized and realized P/L, top movers, and a smooth interactive line chart of portfolio value over selectable time ranges.

🥧

Allocation by Asset Class

A custom-rendered donut chart breaks down your allocation by equities, crypto, ETFs, bonds, real estate, cash, commodities, and other.

📈

Holdings

Sortable, searchable position lists with per-position sparklines, average cost, market value, and P/L percent.

📷

Receipts & Transactions

Each holding opens to a detail view with full transaction history and any attached receipt photos from camera or photo library.

🔬

Deep Analytics

Monthly P/L bars, monthly dividend income, monthly transaction volume, and risk readouts driven entirely by your own recorded returns.

Seven Calculators

ROI, dividend yield, DCA, compound growth, volatility, cost basis, and goal tracker — each with a clear formula breakdown.

🔒

On-Device Only

No execution, no custody, no live market data, no account, no signup, and no server. Everything lives on-device.

📤

Your Data, Exportable

Encrypted at rest by iOS. Export your full journal as JSON whenever you want — your data is always yours.

Privacy first

KoreGrid does not collect, share, or transmit personal data to any server. All journal entries remain on your device.
